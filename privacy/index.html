Energize Solutions Inc. / Website Privacy

Privacy and responsible information handling.

This notice explains how information may be handled when you visit energizeos.com, review its engineering content or contact Energize Solutions Inc. about energy management software, microgrid integration and technical research.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Information you choose to share

When you contact us by email, you may provide your name, email address, organization, project details and information included in messages or attachments. We use business correspondence to evaluate the inquiry, respond, discuss requested services and maintain relevant communication records. Please avoid sending passwords or sensitive operational credentials through an ordinary email inquiry.

2. Website access and technical information

Like other websites, this site depends on hosting and network infrastructure. Providers involved in serving the site may process technical information associated with requests, which can include an IP address, browser and device details, requested pages and time or error information. Such information may be used for delivery, reliability, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. Specific infrastructure log practices are governed in part by the providers involved.

3. Service providers and independent websites

Business email and website hosting involve service providers that may process information while delivering those services. Links to external technical documentation, professional networks and source publications take you to websites operated by other organizations. Their privacy notices and data practices are separate from this notice.

4. Cookies and advertising

These corporate pages are primarily informational. If advertising services, measurement technology or additional cookie-based features are introduced, the relevant information handling and any applicable consent choices will be described before those features are used. Advertising technology providers, including Google if selected, may use cookies or other identifiers to serve and measure ads and may offer choices about personalized advertising. Details of how Google uses information from partner sites are available at Google's partner-site information page.

5. Retention and safeguards

Business correspondence may be retained where it is relevant to an ongoing inquiry, customer relationship, legitimate recordkeeping requirement or legal obligation. Retention and deletion can depend on the subject of the correspondence and the systems used. No public website can guarantee absolute security, so we encourage you not to transmit confidential system credentials through this contact channel.

6. International visitors and privacy requests

Website infrastructure and business correspondence may involve processing in more than one country. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to or correction or deletion of information concerning you. For a question about this notice or a privacy request, contact info@energizeos.com. Requests are reviewed in light of applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Updates

We may update this notice when our website, third-party services or applicable requirements change. The last-updated date above identifies the version shown on this page.

For general business questions, visit our Contact page. Engineering material on this website is informational and subject to project-specific verification.
